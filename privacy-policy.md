# Foodie Privacy Policy

_Effective October 15, 2026_

Foodie is a recipe, meal-planning and grocery app made by Finlay Turnquist ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Foodie stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: **finn.foodieapp@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- **No ads, no tracking, no selling.** Foodie has no advertising or analytics tracking, doesn't follow you across other apps or websites, and never sells or rents your information.
- **Your data is used to run the app**: syncing your recipes and plans between your devices, sharing with the household and friends you choose, and showing recipes from the community.
- **A lot happens only on your phone.** Receipt scanning, voice commands while cooking, and Foodie's smart features (like recipe descriptions and time estimates) run on your device. Their photos, audio and text aren't sent to us.
- **You can delete your account and data at any time** in Settings › Delete Account.

## What Foodie stores, and why

### Your account
When you sign in (with Apple or with email and password), we store your **email address** (or the private relay address Apple gives us if you choose "Hide My Email"), a **user ID**, and the **username** you choose. Your username is visible to other Foodie users so friends can find you. Signing in is optional for most of the app; it's needed for syncing, households, friends, comments and ratings.

### Your recipes, plan, pantry and shopping list
Your **cookbook** (recipes you save, import or write, and photos you add to them), **meal plan**, **pantry**, **shopping list** and **diet and goals** are stored on your phone and, when you're signed in, backed up to your Foodie account so they sync between your devices. Only you can see them, unless you share them in a household (below).

### Households
If you create or join a household, the people in it share a **meal plan, pantry and shopping list**. A household also holds a profile for each person you plan meals for: their **name**, an optional emoji, **diet, allergies and foods they'd rather avoid**, and **nights they're away**. These are used to keep unsafe recipes out of suggestions and to scale servings. You can add profiles for people who don't use the app, such as children; that information is entered by you and seen only by your household. Meals you mark "just me" stay private to you.

**Send to Family** stores the week's dinners as sent, each person's veto ("not this week") until the week locks, and the household's wish list of requests. They're visible only to the household, and are deleted with it. After dinner, everyone's reaction to it (😍, 🙂 or 😖, including for profiles you manage) is kept as the household's **taste memory**, so later suggestions bring back favourites and leave out what people disliked. Your reactions are removed if you leave.

**Pick Dinners Together** stores the shared deck of recipes and each person's swipes (likes and passes) so the household can see the top picks. Each new deck replaces the last one.

### Friends, posts, comments and ratings
- **Friends:** your friend list and friend requests are visible only to you and the other person.
- **Posts:** meals you share (with any photo and caption) are visible to you and your friends. Hearts on a post are seen by the same people, with how many there are.
- **Recipe comments:** comments you leave on a recipe are visible to **everyone signed in to Foodie**, with your username. You can delete your own comments at any time. Others can report a comment; reported comments may be hidden and are reviewed by us.
- **Ratings:** your own star ratings are private to you. Foodie shows each recipe's **average** rating and number of ratings to everyone, without saying who rated it.
- **Taste Test (beta versions only):** testers' 😋 and 👎 votes on recipes are private to them. Foodie keeps each recipe's vote totals, without saying who voted, to decide which recipes stay in the app.
- **Blocking and reports:** if you block someone or report content, we store that so we can act on it.

### The community catalog
When you import a recipe from a website, Foodie may share **the link** to it (not your copy) so other cooks can discover it. Our server reads that public page and keeps the recipe's facts: name, site, photo link, times, servings, ingredients, and categories. Never the method or the author's photos. **Who imported it isn't stored**: we keep only a one-way scrambled code so the same person isn't counted twice. You can turn this off in Settings ("Share recipes I import"). Recipes from sites that don't allow sharing are never added to the catalog and stay only in your cookbook.

### Notifications
If you allow notifications, we store your device's **push notification token** to send things like household dinner updates and cooking reminders. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

## What stays on your phone

- **Receipt scanning:** the camera image and the text read from it are processed on your device to add items to your pantry. The receipt image isn't uploaded.
- **Voice commands while cooking:** speech is recognized on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition.
- **Smart features** such as recipe descriptions and cooking-time estimates use Apple's on-device models.
- **Camera and photo access** is used only when you choose to take or pick a photo (for a post, a recipe, or a receipt). A photo is uploaded only if you add it to a post or a recipe.

## Services we use

We share information only with the services needed to run Foodie:

- **Google Firebase** stores your account and app data, sends push notifications, and runs our servers. [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Apple** handles Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications.
- **TheMealDB** provides many recipes. Searches go through our own server and contain no personal information.
- **Recipe websites:** when you import or open a recipe from a website, your phone loads that page from the site, as a browser would. Our catalog server also reads public recipe pages. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to their pages.
- **Grocery stores** (Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Target): if you tap to shop or share your list with a store, you go to that store's app or website, and its privacy policy applies. Foodie doesn't receive your orders or payment details.

## How long we keep it, and deleting it

- **Delete your account** in Settings › Delete Account. This removes your account and the data tied to it: your synced cookbook, plan, pantry and lists, your posts and photos, your comments, your ratings and Taste Test votes (totals are recounted without them), your reports, your friendships and requests, and your notification tokens. You leave any household; your diet profile is removed from it.
- **Leaving a household** removes your profile and swipes from it. When the last person leaves, the household and its shared data are deleted.
- **Catalog facts** about public recipe pages aren't personal information and may remain after you delete your account, with no link to you.
- **Backups** held by our hosting provider may take a short time to expire after deletion.

## Children

Foodie is not directed to children under 13, and children shouldn't create accounts. A parent or guardian can add a profile for a child (a name, an emoji and dietary needs) to plan the household's meals. That profile has no account and is visible only to the household. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that let only the right people see it (you, your household, or your friends). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app, and delete it as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to how it's used. Email **finn.foodieapp@gmail.com** and we'll help.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
